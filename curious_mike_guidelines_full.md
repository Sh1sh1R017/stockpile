### The goal
  Get Curious Mike in front of people who have never heard of it, and get them arguing in the comments.
  This is not a views-at-any-cost campaign. What the show wants out of it:
  - New listeners. Someone sees your clip, has an opinion about it, goes and finds the full episode. That is the win.
  - Comment sections that move. A clip with 50,000 views and a fight in the replies is worth more to the show than a clip with 200,000 passive views. That is why the 1% engagement rule exists.
  - The right audience. US, Canada and UK viewers are the ones who follow the NBA and the guests. That is why the 40% geography rule exists.
  - Clips the show would be happy to repost. Clean edits, correct spelling, nothing that makes MPJ or his guests look bad in a way they did not intend.

> [!NOTE]
> 
    Every rule on this page comes back to one of those four things. If you are ever unsure whether something is allowed, ask which of the four it serves. If the answer is none of them, it is probably a no.

  Every clip you make gets posted one time. That is it.

### The 6 rules, and how to actually pass them
  Every clip has to pass all 6. Miss one and the clip pays nothing, it does not get partial credit. Each rule below has a how to pass it section, because the rules are not the hard part, hitting them is.

#### Rule 1: At least 40% of viewers from US, Canada or UK
  Combined, not each. So 25% US + 10% Canada + 8% UK = 43%, that passes.
  How to pass it:
  - Post between 12pm and 9pm Eastern. That is when the US is awake and scrolling.
  - Keep the hook text in English, no other languages on screen.
  - Use American b roll. NBA arenas, US sports media, American memes.
  - During your warm up, follow and engage with US and UK accounts. Your first 100 viewers come from accounts like yours, and the algorithm copies that pattern forever.
  - Set your account region to your real country and do not use a VPN.
  - Never buy views. Bought views come from wherever is cheapest and they will tank this number instantly.

#### Rule 2: At least 1% engagement rate
  Engagement = (likes + comments) ÷ views. On a 100,000 view clip you need 1,000 likes and comments combined.
  How to pass it:
  - Clip an argument. This is the single biggest lever. On the official page, the clips with a fight in them hit 3.5% to 4.2%. The clips that were just entertaining hit 1.0%. Same show, same editor, 4x difference.
  - Ask a question in your caption. "he's wrong right?" gets comments. "crazy clip" gets nothing.
  - Pin a comment with an opinion the second you post. Give people something to reply to.
  - Reply to every comment in the first hour. Replies count as comments and they push more replies.
  - Clip a ranking or a take, not a story. "Who is the best shooter" makes everyone answer. "Here is my childhood" makes nobody answer.

#### Rule 3: No AI generated video
  Not allowed: AI avatars, AI generated b roll, AI upscaling, AI extended footage, AI faceless narration.
  Allowed: AI captions and AI auto transcription for subtitles. That is a tool, not content.
  How to pass it: use real footage. That is it.

#### Rule 4: At least 2,000 views
  How to pass it:
  - Warm the page up before you post clips. A cold account gets capped at 200 views for weeks.
  - The first half second decides everything. If your hook is weak the clip dies at 300 views no matter how good the rest is.
  - Post at consistent times so the algorithm knows when to test you.
  - If a clip dies, do not delete it. Just post the next one.

#### Rule 5: Only the 2 approved episodes
  Clips from any other Curious Mike episode pay nothing. The approved list is in the next section.

#### Rule 6: The YT: @mpj watermark on every clip

> [!NOTE]
> 
    This is not optional and it is not "most clips". It is every single clip, every platform, every time.
    A clip with 400,000 views and no watermark pays $0. Check it before you export, not after you post.
  Why it exists: the whole point of the campaign is sending people to the full episodes. Someone watches your clip, wants more, and the watermark tells them exactly where to go. Without it the view is wasted.
  The spec:
  - Use the supplied watermark file. Do not type it out yourself, do not recreate it in your editor, do not change the font.
  - On screen for the entire clip. Not just the first 3 seconds, not just the last 3 seconds.
  - Keep it legible. It is white with a soft glow, so it needs to sit on footage it can actually be read against. If a shot is bright white behind it, move it or pick a different cutaway.
  - Do not stretch it, recolour it, rotate it, add effects to it, or crop any part of it off frame.
  - Do not cover it with captions, a hook, a progress bar or b roll.
  Where to put it: you have a lot of freedom here. Anywhere on the frame is fine except the four corners, and anywhere your caption sits. The bottom of the frame is good, as long as the caption does not cover it.

  | Table:
    | ✅ GOOD | ❌ NO |
    | Middle of the frame, high or low | Any of the four corners |
    | Bottom of the frame, clear of the caption | Anywhere the caption overlaps it |
    | Top area, under the status bar | Half on, half off the edge of the frame |
    | The same spot on every clip you post | Moving it around clip to clip |

> [!NOTE]
> 
    Two things worth knowing when you pick your spot. Neither is a rule, just what saves you a re-edit.
    On TikTok and Reels the like, comment and share buttons run up the right side of the screen. If you place the watermark low, keep it to the left of those.
    The caption block sits lower on some platforms than others. Preview your first clip in each app, find a spot that clears the caption everywhere, then use that same spot from then on.
  How to pass it: build it into your template once. Drop the watermark on a locked layer in your editing project, save that project as your starting template, and every clip you make from then on has it automatically. That is the whole fix, and it takes two minutes.

### Clip formats that work
  Pick one of these. Do not invent a new format on this campaign.

#### Format 1: Straight talking head
  The idea: the clip is the conversation. No tricks.
  How to make it:
  - Find the sharpest 30 seconds in the episode
  - Cut so the first words you hear are the punchline, not the question
  - Big burned in captions, word by word
  - Text hook across the top for the whole clip
  - Post it
  Best for: beginners, and for the strongest moments that do not need help.

#### Format 2: Talking head + b roll
  The idea: same as above, but you cut away to footage of whatever is being talked about.
  How to make it:
  - Start with the talking head so people know what they are watching
  - When he says a name or a place, cut to it on that word, not three seconds later
  - Keep the cutaway to 1 to 3 seconds, then come back to the podcast
  - Podcast audio runs the whole time, never cut to the b roll audio
  - Never cover the speaker for more than a third of the clip
  Best for: anything about a specific player, team, game or arena.

#### Format 3: Question on screen, answer in the clip
  The idea: you put the question as text and cut straight to the answer.
  How to make it:
  - Text on screen: "Who's the best shooter in the NBA?"
  - Cut out Mike asking it entirely
  - First audio is the guest answering
  - Keeps the whole clip payoff with zero setup
  Best for: rankings, hot takes, anything where the question itself is the hook.

> [!NOTE]
> 
    Formats that do not get paid on this campaign: reaction cams, your own commentary, voiceover explainers, AI narration, text-to-speech, photo slideshows.

### Warming up a new page

> [!NOTE]
> 
    Do not skip this. If you make a fresh account and immediately post 5 clips, the algorithm caps you at 200 views a video and it can take a month to recover. One week of warm up saves you a month of dead posts.

#### Days 1 to 3: just be a normal user
  - [ ] Fill out the whole profile. Picture, bio, name. A half finished profile looks like a bot.
  - [ ] Use your normal wifi or mobile data. No VPN.
  - [ ] Watch the niche for 20 to 30 minutes a day. Podcast clips, NBA content, Curious Mike.
  - [ ] Watch videos all the way through and rewatch a few. Watch time is the strongest signal you can send.
  - [ ] Scroll past anything off topic fast. Every second on an unrelated video confuses the algorithm about what your account is.
  - [ ] Like 10 to 20 posts a day in the niche.
  - [ ] Leave 3 to 5 real comments a day. Actual sentences, not emojis.
  - [ ] Follow 10 to 20 accounts in the niche. Other podcast pages, NBA pages, the guests, the show.

#### Days 4 to 6: start posting, quietly
  - [ ] Keep watching and engaging every day.
  - [ ] Post 1 or 2 low stakes clips. Normal hook, no link, nothing aggressive.
  - [ ] Reply to any comment you get, fast.
  - [ ] Do not delete posts. Deleting early posts on a new account is a red flag.

#### Day 7 onward: go
  - [ ] Start posting campaign clips, 1 to 4 a day.
  - [ ] Spread them out. One at 9am, one at 2pm, one at 8pm beats 3 at once.
  - [ ] Keep engaging with the niche daily. An account that only posts and never watches gets throttled.
  - [ ] Week 2: 1 to 2 a day. Week 3: 3 to 4 a day if views are holding.

> [!NOTE]
> 
    Things that kill a new page: 10 clips on day one · a VPN or shared IP · editing your bio 5 times in a day · buying followers · going quiet for a week then posting a burst · posting the same edit twice

### FAQ
  [toggle] Can I post the same clip on all 3 platforms?
    No. One clip, one post. If you want to use the same moment again you have to properly re-edit it into a different clip, and changing the font, the colour or trimming the ends does not count. See the One clip, one post section.
  [toggle] I posted a clip and forgot the watermark. Can I still submit it?
    No. The clip does not qualify no matter how it performed. Do not delete it, deleting posts hurts your page. Just add the watermark to your template so it cannot happen again, and post the next one.
  [toggle] Can I make the watermark smaller or move it somewhere subtle?
    You can choose where it sits as long as it stays legible and is not covered by captions or platform buttons. You cannot resize it down until it is unreadable, recolour it, or add effects. If a reviewer has to squint at it, it fails.
  [toggle] Can I repost a clip from the Curious Mike page?
    No, and this is the one that gets people removed from the campaign. Taking a video off @curiousmike or @mpj and reposting it is not clipping. You edit your own clips from the full episode.
  [toggle] My clip got 50,000 views but only 0.8% engagement. Do I get anything?
    No. The 1% floor is a floor, not a target. Leave the clip up, it does not count against you, but it will not be paid. Next time clip something more divisive.
  [toggle] My views are still going up. Submit now or wait?
    Wait until it slows down. Whatever number you submit is what gets paid, and clips do not get re reviewed later for a bigger number.
  [toggle] How many clips can I post?
    As many as you want. There is no volume cap. The only caps are $300 per clip and the shared $7,500 budget. Posting more clips is the only way to scale what you make.
  [toggle] Can I use my personal account?
    If it already has a real audience in the podcast or NBA niche, yes. If it is your personal account with friends and family on it, build a separate fan page. The algorithm will not push podcast clips out of an account it has already decided is a personal account.
  [toggle] Does episode 2 replace the Trae Young episode?
    No. Both stay live for the whole campaign. When episode 2 drops on September 30 you can clip either one.
  [toggle] Can I add my own commentary or a reaction cam?
    No. Clips are the show's footage with captions, hooks and b roll. Reaction content is a different format and does not get paid here.
  [toggle] What if my clip gets taken down for copyright?
    Post it in #content-rewards and we will sort it out. Do not delete your account or mass delete posts.
  [toggle] How long until I get paid?
    24 to 48 hours after you send your analytics. Each clip is paid on approval, one at a time, so you are not waiting on the rest of the campaign or on anyone else's clips.

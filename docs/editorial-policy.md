# Editorial policy

## Relevance-first B-roll

Stockpile treats B-roll relevance as the objective, not B-roll coverage.

A candidate is inserted only when it passes editorial eligibility and relevance gates. Coverage percentages are telemetry, not quotas and never a reason to invent, move, stretch, or pad a shot.

The Director timeline audit may reject malformed or out-of-contract intervals. It must not fill gaps, extend approved holds, shift approved intervals to hit coverage, or create placeholder visuals.

When no candidate passes, Stockpile stays on A-roll.

Reference-style campaigns may request more visual opportunities, but semantic relevance and the timing contract remain authoritative.
